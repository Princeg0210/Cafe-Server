# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Integrated Café Management, Digital Ordering, Reservation & Operations Management System

---

## 1. Product Overview & Vision
The **Integrated Café Management, Digital Ordering, Reservation & Operations Management System** is an end-to-end hospitality platform designed to unify guest-facing mobile ordering, table reservations, POS billing, multi-kitchen operations, and backend inventory control into a single real-time ecosystem.

---

## 2. Core Objectives
1. **Digitize Reservations:** Provide an online scheduling portal with automated availability checks and capacity capping.
2. **Table QR Ordering:** Enable context-aware, table-specific QR code scanning for digital menu browsing and direct cart submission.
3. **Transparent Running Bills:** Offer real-time non-payment itemized bill viewing at the table.
4. **Centralized POS Settlement:** Retain all financial payment processing strictly at the physical cashier counter.
5. **Dual-Kitchen KDS Routing:** Automatically route mixed orders item-by-item to Kitchen 1 (Hot Food) and Kitchen 2 (Beverages/Bar).
6. **Inventory & Procurement:** Track ingredient stock levels automatically via recipe BOM rules and trigger low-stock alerts.
7. **Owner Analytics & Feedback:** Provide executive KPI dashboards and automated Google Review feedback routing.

---

## 3. Scope Boundaries
* **In Scope (MVP):** Reservation portal, capacity control, table QR session binding, digital menu, cart, running bill display, POS terminal checkout, dual-kitchen KDS, thermal printing, basic inventory master, low-stock alerts, and owner analytics.
* **Future Scope:** Loyalty reward wallets, self-service kiosks, multi-branch expansion, AI predictive forecasting, and native mobile apps.
